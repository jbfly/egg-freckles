EF8 routeScripts install sweep and uninstall scope
===================================================

Instance: ef8round (seeded, isolated).

Before install, Notes held two stock routes, a non-AI third-party marker, and a
stale earlier-generation AI route:

    "len=4 |stock |stock |Third Party:KEEP |Old AI:AI"

After installing EggFrecklesEF8:jbfly:

    "len=5 |stock |stock |Third Party:KEEP |Convert to Text:AI=EggFrecklesEF8:jbfly:text:install:icon=frame |Ask AI:AI=EggFrecklesEF8:jbfly:ask:install:icon=frame"

Exactly the two fresh EF8 AI entries remain. Both stock entries and
Third Party:KEEP are unchanged.

Immediately before actual SafeRemovePackage:

    "len=5 |stock |stock |Third Party:KEEP |Convert to Text:AI=EggFrecklesEF8:jbfly |Ask AI:AI=EggFrecklesEF8:jbfly"

After uninstall:

    "removed"
    "len=3 |stock |stock |Third Party:KEEP"

RemoveScript therefore removed both EF8 entries and nothing else. Screenshot
ef8round-11-after-uninstall.png shows the stock envelope menu without Convert
to Text or Ask AI.
