EF8 Convert to Text and Ask AI results
======================================

All three actions were real taps in the stock Notes envelope menu on isolated
instance ef8round. Replies landed through the existing FileReply path as native
text notes filed in AI.

1. Handwriting -> transcription

The stock Notes recognizer stored the four drawn H/I strokes as one paragraph:

    "uid=3 n=1 stat=para cls=frame text=HI"

Convert to Text sent:

    INK BODY mode=text bytes=30 strokes=0 points=0
    "POST /ink HTTP/1.0" 200

The filed reply was usable text:

    "uid=4 label=AI text=re: HI

    HI"

Screenshots: ef8round-02-handwriting.png, ef8round-03-handwriting-menu.png,
ef8round-04-handwriting-result.png.

2. Drawing -> description

A house drawn with the stock Sketches recognizer was native ink2:

    "uid=5 n=1 ink=ink2"

Convert to Text sent mode=text and the deterministic transcription/description
prompt:

    INK BODY mode=text bytes=532 strokes=6 points=102 bytes_per_point=5.22
    INK PROMPT '... saying what is drawn; if it is writing, transcribe it. ...'
    "POST /ink HTTP/1.0" 200

The filed reply was:

    "uid=6 label=AI text=re: 6 strokes

    A simple outline of a house with a short vertical line inside."

Screenshots: ef8round-05-drawing.png, ef8round-06-drawing-menu.png,
ef8round-07-drawing-result.png.

3. Ask AI -> existing conversational reply path

A triangle drawn with the same stock Sketches tool was native ink2:

    "uid=7 n=1 ink=ink2"

Ask AI sent mode=ask and the conversational prompt:

    INK BODY mode=ask bytes=931 strokes=3 points=197 bytes_per_point=4.73
    INK PROMPT '... Respond to what the note says or asks ...'
    "POST /ink HTTP/1.0" 200

The answer used the same FileReply/AI-folder behaviour as the former Send to AI:

    "uid=8 label=AI text=re: 3 strokes

    That looks like a neatly drawn triangle."
