3. The keys land in OUR input line
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Host key injection does NOT reach Einstein — `emulator.client key BackSpace`
and `emulator.client text "Z"` both left the field byte-identical. So the
keyboard was driven the way the human will: by tapping its keys.

Tapped the keyboard's "del" key 11 times (285,308):
$ runtime/ns_eval.py --container newton-harness-loaderround_emulator_1 \
    'begin local v := GetRoot().|-Loader1:jbfly|.filenameView; "text=[" & v.text & "]" end'
"text=[]"

Then tapped h e l l o . p k g on the on-screen keyboard:
$ runtime/ns_eval.py --container newton-harness-loaderround_emulator_1 \
    'begin local v := GetRoot().|-Loader1:jbfly|.filenameView; "text=[" & v.text & "]" end'
"text=[hello.pkg]"

Screenshot: loaderround-typed.png (field reads "hello.pkg", keyboard up)

That is the whole feature proven: tapping the ROM keyboard's keys edits the
loader's filename field, with no ink written at all.

5. Two things observed, neither introduced by this round
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
a) `SuckPackageFromBinary`'s progress callback never fires for a package
   smaller than its `callbackFrequency: 8192`, so `installedIdentity` stays
   nil and the status reads "Install not confirmed" even though the install
   SUCCEEDED — proven above by GetPkgRef. Only bites packages under 8 KiB;
   every real payload (egg-freckles.pkg is 19 KB) is larger. Left alone
   deliberately: this round was not to touch the install machinery.

b) A NewtonOS paragraph breaks on CR, not LF, so an embedded "\n" draws as an
   empty-box glyph. Visible in the status text in loaderround-installed.png
   ("X[]n=evt.ex.comm[]e=-16009..."). The window header used to have the same
   box between the title and "File:"; that one is fixed here by using two
   protoStaticText children instead of one string with "\n". The status
   strings belong to the install machinery and were left alone.

c) Extras' "All Icons" view sorts by the displayed label, not by package
   identity: "Loader" appears under L, between "Internet Setup" and
   "Minico:Scrawl". The leading "-" in the identity is kept as the
   established convention, but this screenshot is evidence it does not by
   itself float the icon to the top of that view.
