--- ns_eval cannot see NTK platform constants -------------------------------

$ runtime/ns_eval.py --instance c4round "... store:HasSoup(ROM_paperRollSoupName) ..."
"EX evt.ex.fr.intrp;type.ref.frame"

ROM_paperRollSoupName is an NTK *compile-time* constant from
~/newton-dev/ntk-platform-files (strings shows ROM_paperRollSoupName,
ROM_paperRollIndices, ROM_paperrollSoupDef there). Injected NewtonScript is not
compiled by NTK, so the name is unbound -> nil -> HasSoup(nil) throws
"expected a frame". Probe with the literal soup name instead; the package source
keeps using the constant.

$ ... 'begin local r := ""; foreach n in GetStores()[0]:GetSoupNames() do r := r & n & ";"; r end'
"Calendar;Calendar Notes;Calls;Directory;InBox;Internet DNS Cache;Internet Setups;Library;Names;Notes;OutBox;Packages;Repeat Meetings;Repeat Notes;System;SystemAlarmSoup;To do;To Do List;"

So ROM_paperRollSoupName == "Notes" on this ROM.

--- cursor:CountEntries() works, and is what note_list counts with -----------

$ ... 'begin local r := "?"; try r := "n=" & GetStores()[0]:GetSoup("Notes"):Query({indexPath: 'timeStamp}):CountEntries() onexception |evt| do r := "EX " & CurrentException().name; r end'
"n=3"

refs/NewtonProgrammerRef20.txt:34215-34243 documents it and warns that counting
a large number of entries is time-consuming; it walks the index, not the
entries, so it is far cheaper than the R10I full-soup entry scan that caused the
twelfth finding's event-loop starvation.

--- every seeded Notepad entry has title = nil ------------------------------

$ ... 'begin local c := GetStores()[0]:GetSoup("Notes"):Query({indexPath: 'timeStamp}); local e := c:Entry(); "cls=" & ClassOf(e) & " ts=" & GetSysEntryData(e, 'timeStamp) & " titlecls=" & ClassOf(GetSysEntryData(e, 'title)) end'
"cls=paperroll ts=64461125 titlecls=weird_immediate"

$ ... walk of all entries: ordinal:timeStamp/ClassOf(title)
"1:64461125/weird_immediate;2:64462106/weird_immediate;3:64464021/weird_immediate;"

weird_immediate is NIL (same rendering as the pkg_list store slot in the
thirteenth finding). A nil title is the normal case, not the exception, which is
why note_list falls back to the note's first characters.

--- and all three of them have data = nil too -------------------------------

$ ... walk of all entries: ordinal:ClassOf(GetSysEntryData(e, 'data))
"1:dataclass=weird_immediate;2:dataclass=weird_immediate;3:dataclass=weird_immediate;"
