EF7 routeScripts sweep — isolated install/uninstall proof
========================================================

Instance: ef7round (seeded, isolated)
Package: EggFrecklesEF7:jbfly / Egg Freckles 1.0-ef7

Before install, the live Notes array was seeded with the two stock entries, a
non-AI third-party marker, and a stale EF6 AI entry:

    "len=4 |stock |stock |Third Party:KEEP |Old AI:AI"

After installing EF7:

    "len=4 |stock |stock |Third Party:KEEP |Send to AI:AI=EggFrecklesEF7:jbfly"
    "item=EggFrecklesEF7:jbfly agent=EggFrecklesEF7:jbfly via=install"

Exactly one AI entry remains. Both stock entries and Third Party:KEEP are
unchanged, and the menu item's live agent is the newly installed EF7 agent.

Actual SafeRemovePackage uninstall, against the same array:

    "before uninstall len=4 |stock |stock |Third Party:KEEP |Send to AI:AI=EggFrecklesEF7:jbfly"
    "removed"
    "after uninstall len=3 |stock |stock |Third Party:KEEP"

Uninstall removed only EF7's entry. It preserved both stock entries and the
third-party non-AI entry.
